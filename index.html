The process, as we understand it so far

This is our current guess at the shape of an investigation. None of it is confirmed by anyone who does this work. If a step is wrong, missing, or in the wrong order — that's exactly what we want to hear below.

i.

Alert arrives

A transaction-monitoring system flags activity against a rule or model.

Unverified
ii.

Investigate

The investigator opens the alert and starts forming a picture of what happened.

Unverified
iii.

Gather evidence

Transaction history, customer profile, counterparties — possibly across more than one system.

Unverified
iv.

Analyse

The investigator forms a view on whether the activity is explainable or needs escalating.

Unverified
v.

Write it up

The reasoning gets documented for the file, and for whoever reviews it next.

Unverified
vi.

Review

Someone else — a second-line reviewer, an MLRO — checks the decision.

Unverified
vii.

Close

The case is closed, escalated further, or reported.
