What actually happens between an AML transaction-monitoring alert arriving and an investigator closing it?

We are not looking for a pitch.

We are researching the real investigation workflow — what investigators actually see, open, search, verify, reconstruct, document and send for review.

No customer names, account numbers, case identifiers or confidential information. We only want the workflow.
